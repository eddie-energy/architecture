## Overview

This section includes the workflows related to revoking the customer's consent for access to their data. This revocation may be triggered by any actor, e.g., the customer, the eligible party, or the metered data administrator. For this reason, these workflows are shown below based on the initiating actor.



| Actor | Section |
|-|-|
| The customer revokes the consent at the Consent Admin Portal | [Link](./revocation-customer-consent-admin-portal/revocation-customer-consent-admin-portal.md) |
| The metered data administrator revokes the consent | [Link](./revocation-meter-data-administrator/revocation-meter-data-administrator.md) |
| The customer revokes the consent by terminating a Service | [Link](./revocation-customer-terminates-service/revocation-customer-terminates-service.md) |
| The eligible party revokes the consent by terminating a Service | [Link](./revocation-eligible-party-terminates-service/revocation-eligible-party-terminates-service.md) |
