[Loxone](https://www.loxone.com/) is a proprietary smart home system for monitoring and controlling IoT devices. Loxone also provides proprietary IoT devices that seamlessly integrate with the Loxone system.

## Connection to Metering Devices

Many Loxone devices, such as smart meters, energy monitors, and power relays, collect energy metering data. Thus, rather than connecting to metering devices, Loxone devices produce energy data on their own.

## Connection to AIIDA

Loxone devices send data to the Loxone Miniserver wirelessly via the proprietary Loxone Air. The data can then be accessed from the Loxone Miniserver via a REST API, or the Loxone Miniserver can be configured to send the data via HTTP or UDP.
