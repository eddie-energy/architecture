The [Sinapsi Alfa](https://www.sinapsitech.it/en/home-alfa/) is an Adapter Device that supports Open Meter 2.0 smart meters which are commonly used in **Italy**. 
<!-- This Adapter Device is shown below. -->

<!-- ![](./figures/sinapsi-alfa.jpg) -->


## Connection to Metering Devices

The Sinapsi Alfa connects to smart meters via a power outlet and power line communication over the CHAIN 2 communication channel defined by the Open Meter 2.0 standard. Once connected, the Sinapsi Alfa reads data from the smart meter, including electricity consumption and production data.

## Connection to AIIDA

To configure the Sinapsi Alfa, the customer can access a configuration web interface via WiFi. Through this interface, the SSinapsi Alfa can be configured to send energy data to AIIDA via MQTT or HTTP.
