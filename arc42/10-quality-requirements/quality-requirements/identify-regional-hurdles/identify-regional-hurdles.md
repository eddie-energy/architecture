---
title: Identify Technical Regional Hurdles
---

## Context

The process of configuring the EDDIE Framework to access the APIs of the Regional Data-sharing Infrastructures, may reveal technical shortcomings, limitations, and constraints that limit the ability of the eligible party to access the data of the customers. Such hurdles needs to be documented and communicated to the community.

## Stimulus
The process of accessing the APIs of the Regional-Sata-sharing Infrastructures reveals minor or major hurdles, i.e., bugs, lack of documentation, missing features.   

## Response
All hurdles are documented. Also, the hurdles become available online to be accessed by the community.
