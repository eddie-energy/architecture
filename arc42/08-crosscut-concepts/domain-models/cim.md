## Definition

<!-- What it is -->

CIM (Common Information Model) is an electric power transmission and distribution standard developed by the electric power industry. It aims to allow application software to exchange information about an electrical network. It has been officially adopted by the International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC). CIM defines a common vocabulary and basic ontology.

## Relevance

<!-- Where it is used, i.e., in what parts of EDDIE -->

For this system, CIM is used as the target model for storing energy data in the EDDIE Framework.
