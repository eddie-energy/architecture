The Data Services Marketplace is one of the software systems of EDDIE, along with AIIDA and the EDDIE Framework. Its primary goal is to centralize all EDDIE-related data services in one platform and simplify the interaction between customers and eligible parties.
The Data Services Marketplace acts as a discovery platform that allows customers to browse, find, and access energy-related data services offered by eligible parties. In this way, the Marketplace enables customers to easily discover and use energy services that suit their needs.
