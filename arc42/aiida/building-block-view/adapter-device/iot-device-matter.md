[Matter](https://csa-iot.org/all-solutions/matter/) is a technical standard of the Connectivity Standards Alliance for smart home and IoT devices. Due to its popularity and support by IoT companies, a growing number of devices support Matter connectivity.

## Connection to Metering Devices

Many IoT devices supporting Matter produce energy metering data, such as smart plugs and light bulbs. Nevertheless, producing energy metering data is not mandatory for all Matter devices. Thus, rather than connecting to external metering devices, Matter devices produce energy data on their own.

## Connection to AIIDA

Matter devices support IPv6 over WiFi, Ethernet, or Thread, and can send data via CoAP.
