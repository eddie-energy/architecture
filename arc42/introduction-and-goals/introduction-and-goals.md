This document serves as a comprehensive guide for understanding the architecture, key characteristics, and interactions of the systems involved in Project EDDIE: the EDDIE Framework, the Administrative Interface for In-house Data Access (AIIDA), and the Marketplace.

Its primary purpose is to provide a structured overview of the technical background and the interplay between these systems, ensuring a shared understanding among all stakeholders. The document captures all essential requirements and architectural goals, forming the basis for developers to design and implement the systems effectively.

By documenting the architecture and its underlying rationale, this documentation aims to align development efforts with the project’s goals and ensure interoperability, scalability, and compliance with relevant standards and regulations.
