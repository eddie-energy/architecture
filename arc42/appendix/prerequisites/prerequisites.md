The EDDIE Framework has to interact with external components in order to gain access to historical validated and real-time energy data. For example, through interactions with the Regional Data-sharing Infrastructures of Member States, the EDDIE Framework can gain access to historical validated energy data. Also, by connecting to AIIDA instances deployed on in-house devices at the customers' sites, the EDDIE Framework can gain access to real-time energy data. To comprehend how the EDDIE Framework works, it is important to understand how the external components operate. For this reason, the sections below provide an overview of the operation of these external components, which can be considered as prerequisites for accessing historical and real-time energy data.
