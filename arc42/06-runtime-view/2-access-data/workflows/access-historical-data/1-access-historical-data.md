## Overview

This section includes the workflows related to the eligible party accessing the historical validated energy consumption data of the customer. Since these workflows can vary per country, the table below shows links with more detailed information for each country. Notably, these workflows focus on accessing the data from the Regional Data-sharing Infrastructure after the customer consent has been requested. The workflow regarding getting the consent of the customer is presented [here](../../../1-request-consent/workflows/request-consent-historical-data/request-consent-historical-data.md).
