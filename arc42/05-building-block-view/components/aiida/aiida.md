## Overview

AIIDA (Administrative Interface for In-house Data Access) is responsible for establishing the consent of the customer regarding access to real-time data from the customer's Smart Meter. Also, for acquiring the real-time data from the Smart Meter and sending it to the [AIIDA Connector](../interoperable-comm/aiida-connector/aiida-connector.md) of the EDDIE Framework.
